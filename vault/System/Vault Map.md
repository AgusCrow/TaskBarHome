# Vault Map

`00 Human/10 Daily Notes`: dated notes and measurements recorded by the user.
`00 Human/30 Projects`: approved requirements, decisions and tasks.
`00 Human/70 Context`: shared business/context information.
`00 Human/80 Templates`: reusable shapes inserted through Templates.
`Machine/Workflows`: supplied job instructions and examples.
`Machine/Outputs`: generated deliverables from tools.
`Machine/Logs`: records of what actually happened.
`System`: operating maps and explanations.

For Client Update, drafts go to `Machine/Workflows/Client Update/output` and activity goes to `Machine/Logs/Client Updates.md`.
For the Premium Create Project Brief action, use `Machine/Outputs/Project Briefs` and `Machine/Logs/Project Briefs.md`.

Preserve Human sources. A draft interpretation does not become an approved decision until reviewed.
